Jointly fine-tune Bi-Layout, Opening Head, and Cross-Scene Matcher. The
complete-room ZInD_matching manifests provide the end-to-end supervision.
Every training step runs raw panoramas through the live Bi-Layout backbone,
then through the live Opening Head and Matcher. Matching and opening losses
therefore backpropagate into the shared panorama model. The frozen feature
cache is used only as a low-weight teacher regularizer; it is never
substituted for the live forward pass. M6-M9 remain analytical/discrete
geometry stages and are evaluated after the learned M1/M2/M4/M5 chain. They
are not differentiable training modules.
